# M10 Physical Diagnostics & Repair

The owner approved this vertical slice. Branch `codex/m10-physical-diagnostics` began at `248990b`, the verified merge of [M9 PR #11](https://github.com/ernismorka/project-spark/pull/11). The local planning and handoff edits were carried onto the branch.

## Player route

1. Complete First Contact and reach T1. Start the field assignment by collecting a handheld multimeter near the terminal. Show the meter on the engineer and as a compact HUD readout. Teach DC voltage relative to a fixed GND reference before the player leaves the service area.
2. Find three neutral, numbered probe pads distributed across accessible board locations. Place the upstream supply pad on a supply trace near T1, the downstream feed pad beside R1, and the divider output pad beside an existing component landmark. Site labels identify electrical nodes. Early guidance names unmeasured pads and gives a bearing or location cue. Each reading requires walking within the pad's interaction range and pressing the probe key or its accessible HUD button. Capture stable pad IDs and display localized volts without leaving movement mode.
3. Use the field notebook to compare measured voltages to the healthy reference. Allow a diagnosis only after all three distinct readings. Ask players to localize the fault by choosing the suspect segment and selecting the upstream/downstream evidence pair. Incorrect choices preserve readings and explain which observation to reconsider. Render the repair site cue only after successful localization.
4. Walk to the revealed trace junction and restore its connection with a deliberate interaction. The board responds immediately with an amber pending-verification cue. Require a new sweep of the three pads and compare both measurement sets in the notebook.
5. On fresh verified readings, illuminate the repaired segment and mark the service circuit restored. Route the player back to T1 for the existing construction, embedded programming and device activation sequence. Preserve the existing terminal diagnostics as an alternate single-player route and preserve the separate cooperative pilot.

## Circuit and command authority

- Use the existing ideal DC solver and the established 5 V source and two 1 kΩ resistors. Model the broken copper feed as separate upstream VCC and downstream feed nodes. In the open state, R1 connects downstream feed to SENSE and R2 connects SENSE to GND, so both downstream nodes are referenced and read 0 V. Restoring ideal continuity maps both physical supply pads to VCC. The repaired solver result is 5 V at both supply pads and 2.5 V at SENSE. The visible decorative PCB remains outside the solved network.
- Put pad IDs, positions, circuit-node mappings and the single repair target in browser-independent game-core data. Keep tool ownership, field phase, before/after evidence and circuit revision serializable in the single-player episode. Derive voltages in command handlers. Use an explicit target ID and validate phase, current player position, height and grounded state. Reject commands with invalid or remote targets, incomplete evidence, and duplicate repairs. Use the same domain proximity selector to drive the UI prompts.
- Derive the netlist from the trace revision. Measure only on accepted interactions. Keep pre-repair readings read-only after restoration and require after-repair readings from the new revision. Verify the fresh sweep against a recomputed result with existing numerical tolerances. Share the commissioning prerequisite while keeping legacy repair completion and cooperative commands valid. After verification, require proximity to T1 before presenting the commissioning report.
- Keep field evidence and classic repair mutually exclusive for the current attempt. Replay, end session and reload reset the meter, circuit topology and evidence. Pause, checkpoint recovery, language changes and WebGL retry preserve them. Test these boundaries explicitly.
