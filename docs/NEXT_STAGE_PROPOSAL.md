The approved roadmap ends at M5. All M0–M5 are merged. These alternatives are product proposals only; they neither redefine milestone numbers nor authorize implementation.

| Option                                       | User value                                                                                              | Technical risk                                                                                                           | Relative effort                                                                                      | Decision evidence needed                                                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A. Improve existing 3D single-player         | Strongest immediate benefit to every first-time player: orientation, readable feedback, pacing and feel | Low–medium when focused on measured playtest issues; representative GPU performance remains uncertain                    | Small–medium for targeted polish; avoid a broad visual rewrite                                       | Consolidated route observations, camera/controls feedback, learning explanations and hardware profiles                    |
| B. Integrate cooperation into main 3D world  | Unifies adventure and the separate pilot; makes shared exploration meaningful                           | Highest: remote entity rendering/lifecycle, authoritative 3D interactions, latency and reconnect across the full mission | Large compared with the bounded pilot                                                                | Evidence that two-player roles are enjoyable; a separately approved authority/synchronization design and rendering budget |
| C. Expand programming/electronic engineering | Adds creative depth and replay value after players understand the current divider/controller            | Medium–high: curriculum, UI complexity, solver subset and execution safety need explicit boundaries                      | Medium–large depending on the chosen model; nonlinear electronics would be a separate major decision | Players can explain current cause/effect; demand for specific tasks rather than additional syntax/components alone        |

Recommended order: A → C → B. First use the consolidated playtest to prioritize focused improvements to the common 3D experience. Next consider a small, explicitly approved engineering challenge if learning clarity supports it; the deterministic local workbench provides a lower-risk foundation than full-world networking. Integrate 3D cooperation after role value and performance are established, because it combines rendering, mission and networking risks. If the playtest establishes cooperation as the main source of engagement, the owner may reprioritize B ahead of C after a design review.

No option starts automatically after the quality PR. The owner must approve a concrete scope, acceptance criteria and milestone naming before any next feature work.

Decision after this proposal: the owner authorized narrowly scoped option A as M6 3D Gameplay Polish after PR #7 merged. See M6_SCOPE.md and VALIDATION-M6.md. Options B/C and any M7 remain unapproved; the recommendation above is historical proposal context.
